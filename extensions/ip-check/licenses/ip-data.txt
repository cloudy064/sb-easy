DB-IP Lite Country and ASN
--------------------------
IP Geolocation by DB-IP: https://db-ip.com/
Copyright DB-IP. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source: https://db-ip.com/db/lite.php

sb-easy converts the free Lite CSV snapshots into a read-only SQLite range
database. It preserves country, ASN and organization fields, and does not add a
risk score. Lite data has reduced coverage and accuracy. Source snapshot dates,
retrieval URLs and checksums are recorded in data/manifest.json.
Country CSV is obtained from the Framasoft mirror of the original file; its
decoded SHA-1 matches the checksum published by DB-IP. ASN CSV is obtained from
the pinned sapics/ip-location-db redistribution, split into IPv4 and IPv6 files.
That derivative has its own recorded SHA-256 hashes, not the publisher's CSV
checksum. Its source license is preserved at:
https://github.com/sapics/ip-location-db/blob/f7f14ed72a79d09440789554eb8955dfef6514fe/dbip-asn/DBIP-LICENSE
The conversion does not imply endorsement by DB-IP.

Tor
---
Tor Project CollecTor observed exit addresses, licensed under CC0:
https://collector.torproject.org/recent/exit-lists/
https://metrics.torproject.org/about.html
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Only observed ExitAddress records are included. Duplicate addresses are merged;
relay fingerprints and other relay addresses are not used as exit evidence.
This snapshot is historical evidence, not a guarantee of present Tor membership.
The timestamp, retrieval URL and SHA-256 are recorded in data/manifest.json.
The dated recent/ endpoint can expire. A compressed copy is preserved in the
source tree's snapshots/ directory, with the original downloaded SHA-256 and
compressed SHA-256 recorded in data-sources.json. A future snapshot requires an
explicit source-lock update.
